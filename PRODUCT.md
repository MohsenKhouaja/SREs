# Product

## Register

product

## Users

On-call SREs and platform engineers investigating controlled production incidents. They work under pressure and need to assess evidence, understand a proposed remediation, and make a safe, accountable decision.

## Product Purpose

SREs brings agent-led incident investigation, live observability evidence, human approval, and final reporting into one evidence-first workspace. It succeeds when an operator can move from signal to a defensible decision without losing the context behind it.

## Brand Personality

Editorial, composed, precise. The product should feel like a highly considered technical publication: calm enough to think in, exact enough to trust, and premium without becoming ornamental.

## Anti-references

Avoid developer-tool density for its own sake, gamer-style operations consoles, generic AI-chat layouts, neon/cyberpunk tropes, oversized metrics, and decorative gradients. Never make risk or status understandable through color alone.

## Design Principles

- Lead with the question or decision an operator must make, then reveal supporting evidence in a clear reading order.
- Edit information aggressively: hierarchy and whitespace should make the critical signal obvious at a glance.
- Treat evidence as a first-class artifact—timestamped, sourceable, and readable without visual noise.
- Use premium craft sparingly: abstract art and polished materials may frame a moment, never compete with operational work.
- Keep simulation, investigation, approval, and report stages visibly distinct while preserving one continuous narrative.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Preserve strong contrast in the light theme; express every status with text and shape in addition to color; support keyboard operation and clear focus states; respect reduced motion; and retain readable evidence at narrow widths and high zoom.
